# About Me

## Name and role
Lucas Banega, Founder at Casa Banega.

## Audience
Founders and CEOs who run their own companies and make the calls on strategy, hiring, and growth. They are busy, skeptical of generic advice, and looking for perspectives they can act on without needing to filter out fluff.

## Topic pillars
- Lujo y estrategia de marca: cómo las marcas de alta gama construyen posicionamiento, exclusividad y demanda
- Branding: decisiones de identidad y comunicación que sostienen el valor percibido de una marca
- CX (experiencia de cliente): cómo el lujo diseña cada punto de contacto para reforzar la marca
- Visual merchandising: cómo el espacio físico y la presentación del producto comunican la propuesta de marca

## Point of view
Most of the advice circulating in this industry is wrong. The consensus playbook gets repeated because it is safe to say, not because it works.

## Brand promise
Honesty. The one thought people should have on seeing this name: this person tells me what others will not.

## Off limits
Politics. No political takes, ever, regardless of the topic or news cycle.
